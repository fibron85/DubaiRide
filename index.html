Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Dubai airport transfers, hotel pick-ups and city tours.

How much is a Dubai airport transfer?

Airport pick-up and drop-off around 20 km is AED 150. To any location around Dubai up to 60 km it is AED 250.

Do you offer Dubai city tours?

Yes. A half day tour around Dubai starts from AED 600 and a full day tour starts from AED 1,100. Your driver is your city tour guide.

What vehicle will I ride in?

A modern Hyundai Staria 6-seater van with luggage space, heated and cooling seats, kept neat and clean.

Do you do hotel pick-up and drop-off?

Yes. We pick up from and drop to hotels, the airport and any tourist location in Dubai.
